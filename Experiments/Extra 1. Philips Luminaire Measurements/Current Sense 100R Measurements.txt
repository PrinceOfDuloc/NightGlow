Characteristic when the 68R // 56R = 30.7R RS1 resistor of BP5218 PMIC is replaced with bigger resistors for the preferred low brightness:



RS1 = 56R:
V_RMS = 230V (doesn't change)
I_RMS = 27mA
Apperant Power = 6.21VA
Real Power = 4.7W (not sure, forgot to record)
Pf = 0.75


RS1 = 68R:
V_RMS = 230V (doesn't change)
I_RMS = 22mA
Apperant Power = 5.06VA
Real Power = 3.86W
Pf = 0.76

RS1 = 100R (final):
V_RMS = 230V (doesn't change)
I_RMS = 16.4mA
Apperant Power = 3.772VA
Real Power = 3W
Pf = 0.8



Note: The test performed after continuously operating the luminaire for several days with its original 30.7 Ω RS1 resistor measured 7 W of real power (41 mA RMS), which differs significantly from the initial measurement of 5.7 W (37 mA RMS). The reason for this variation is unknown.
